Therefore, we systematically investigated the effect of varying M/E ra­
tios (6:1, 3:1, 1:1, 1:3) on the reaction performance, as shown in Figure. 
S1B. With increasing M/E ratio, methanol conversion decreases while 
ethanol conversion rises, attributed to the reduced ethanol concentra­
tion in the feed stream. The C3+OH selectivity exhibits a volcano-type 
dependence, reaching its maximum at a M/E ratio of 3:1. The selectiv­
ities toward propanol, isopropanol, and pentanol increased, accompa­
nied by reduced butanol formation. The observed enhancement in cross- 
condensation ratio clearly demonstrates a preference for methanol- 
ethanol cross-condensation over ethanol self-condensation pathways. 
However, further increasing M/E ratio, the amount of ethanol involved 
in cross-coupling and self-condensation reduces, leading to the 
decreasing space-time yield of C3+OH. Based on the above results, we 
keep the M/E ratio of 3:1 and LHSV of 3.76 as the optimal condition for 
this catalytic system.

ture. Therefore, the influence of temperature on catalytic performance 
over Ni30Ce/Mg2Al-LDO was studied. As shown in Fig. 1A, with the

ethanol) shows a linear increase and the conversion of ethanol increases 
from 16.0 % to 34.3 %, but the selectivity for C3+ alcohols decreases to 
49.6 % due to the generation of gaseous products, such as CO, CO2 and 
olefins. According to the literature, dehydrogenation is an endothermic

active sites leads to the production of CO, CO2 and CH4 while promoting 
ethanol dehydrogenation, reducing the total carbon efficiency of the 
reaction. Then some researchers tried to add the second element through 
adjusting the electronic structure of Ni to improve the higher alcohols 
selectivity of catalysts. Wu et al. achieved 93.8 % higher alcohol selec­
tivity according to restrict the cracking of C–C bond over Ni-based 
catalyst by adding Sn [37]. Additionally, CeO2 with abundant basic 
sites has been used as an active catalyst to promote the aldol conden­
sation. Li et al. found that adding CeO2 not only favored the aldol 
condensation but also improved the dispersion of metal due to the strong 
interaction of metal and CeO2 species over M-CeO2/AC(M=Cu, Fe, Co, 
Ni, Pd) [28,41]. Furthermore, Wang et al. also reported that the inter­
action of Ni and CeO2 impacted the electronic structure of Ni over 
NiCeO2@C catalysts which achieved a 89.7 % selectivity towards 
C4–8OH [42].
Combining the virtue of MgAl layered double hydroxides and NiCe 
composites, a series of NixCe/Mg2Al-LDO catalysts were synthesized by 
co-impregnation method in the present work. The surface properties, 
geometric structure and electronic structure of NixCe/Mg2Al-LDO were 
characterized using BET, XRD, TEM, NH3/CO2-TPD and XPS. In-situ 
DRIFTS was employed to elucidate the mechanism between the sur­
face properties and catalytic performance. According to the character­
ization results, Mg2Al-LDO provided a high surface area for metal 
dispersion of and numerous strong basic and acidic sites. Adding Ce can 
not only adjust the electronic structure of Ni, but also regulate the acid- 
base distribution, synergistically accelerating the dehydrogenation and 
C–C coupling reaction, therby achieving high-efficiency conversion to 
C3+OH under mild reaction conditions.
