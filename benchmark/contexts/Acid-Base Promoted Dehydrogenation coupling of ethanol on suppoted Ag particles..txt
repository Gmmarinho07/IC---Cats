ABSTRACT 
Dehydrogenation coupling of ethanol to n-butanol has received much attention due to the wide 
application of n-butanol. Highly selective butanol production from ethanol remains a challenge 
due to competitive reactions in the dehydrogenation and/or condensation and great difficulty in 
ethanol dehydrogenation. This work reports a synergistic catalysis between layered double 
hydroxide (LDO), an acidic-basic support, and supported Ag particles for the dehydrogenation 
coupling of ethanol to n-butanol. A selectivity of up to 77 % toward n-butanol with ethanol 
conversion of 23.2 %, amongst impressive levels reported till now, has been achieved at 350 oC, 
0.1 MPa. A selectivity of up to 60 % toward n-butanol with ethanol conversion of 45 %, the

2.2 Preparation
MgAl-LDHs, Mg(OH)2 or AlO(OH): MgAl-LDHs with varied Mg/Al molar ratios, Mg(OH)2, 
and AlO(OH) were prepared following the procedure reported in our previous work55. 
Ag+ loaded MgAl-LDHs, Mg(OH)2, or AlO(OH) : Ag+/MgAl-LDHs, Ag+/Mg(OH)2, and 
Ag+/AlO(OH) were prepared by incipient wetness impregnation of MgAl-LDHs, Mg(OH)2, and 
AlO(OH) with AgNO3 solution, following the procedure reported in our previous work55. 
Ag loaded MgAl-LDO, Al2O3, and MgO: The prepared Ag+/MgAl-LDHs was calcined at 500 
oC for 1 h under N2 atmosphere with a heating rate of 5 oC min-1, affording Ag/Mg2Al-LDO, 
Ag/Mg3Al-LDO, and Ag/Mg4Al-LDO with a Ag loading of 0.68 % in each case. For a better 
comparison, Ag/Al2O3 or Ag/MgO was prepared by calcination of Ag+/AlO(OH) and 
Ag+/Mg(OH)2 under N2 atmosphere under a calcination temperature reduced to 400 °C while 
with the reduction time prolonged to 3 h, to ensure a similar size distribution of Ag particles in 
Ag/Al2O3 or Ag/MgO to that in Ag/MgAl-LDO. The Ag loadings were determined by ICP 
technique between 0.73 wt% and 0.64 wt%.

Mg/Al ratio of 4.15 under N2 atmosphere at 500 oC for 1 h, and Al2O3 and MgO were prepared 
by calcination of AlO(OH) and Mg(OH)2 under N2 atmosphere at 400 oC for 3 h. The

2.4 Catalytic test
The coupling of ethanol were performed in a fixed-bed reactor with a stainless steel tubular 
reactor (10 mm external diameter, 38 cm length) equipped with an on-line gas chromatography 
(GC). Typically, 0.5 g of sieved catalyst (20-40 mesh) was loaded into the reactor and the 
remaining was filled with quartz sand (20-40 mesh). Prior to the reaction, the catalyst was 
pretreated in situ with N2 (40 mL min-1) at 400 oC for 3 h. Ethanol vapor and N2 were mixed into 
the reaction system after the reactor was cooled to 350 oC. The N2 gas flow was set to 60 mL 
min-1. Before admission of ethanol into the reaction system, ethanol was vaporized at 150 oC in a 
vaporizing chamber. The chromatographically pure ethanol was pumped into the vaporizing 
chamber at 50 μL min-1 using a high-performance liquid chromatography pump. The pipeline 
behind the reactor was heated to keep at 200 oC. The products were identified by GC-MS 
(Agilent 7200 Q-TOF GC/MS) and analyzed quantitatively by GC (Shimadzu, 2014C) with a 
flame ionization detector (FID) and a GSBP-INOWAX column (30 m, 0.25 mm inner diameter).

========== RESULTS ==========

RESULTS AND DISCUSSION
Figure 1 shows the XRD patterns of as-prepared hydroxides, Ag loaded hydroxides, and Ag 
loaded oxides. The characteristic reflections of hydrotalcite-like structure are clearly observed in 
the XRD patterns of MgxAl-LDHs (Figure 1A, a-c). The characteristic reflections of bohmite 
structure for AlO(OH) (JCPDS: 21-1307) (Figure 1A, d) and brucite structure for Mg(OH)2 
(JCPDS: 44-1482) (Figure 1A, e) are also clearly. In each case, no reflections for other phases 
are observed (Figure 1A). No phase transformation is observed after impregnation with AgNO3 
solution (Figure 1B). By calcination of Ag loaded hydroxides under N2, the resulting Ag/MgxAl-
LDO (Figure 1C, a-c) display both of Mg-Al mixed oxide (MgxAl-LDO) phase (JCPDS: 45-
0946) and metallic Ag phase (JCPDS: 04-0783). The characteristic reflections of Al2O3 (JCPDS: 
04-0880) (Figure 1C, d) and MgO (Figure 1C, e) can be observed. Also the reflections for Ag 
phase were clearly for Ag/Al2O3 (Figure 1C, d) and Ag/MgO (Figure 1C, e). The Ag loading 
was determined by ICP technique between 0.73 wt% and 0.64 wt%.
