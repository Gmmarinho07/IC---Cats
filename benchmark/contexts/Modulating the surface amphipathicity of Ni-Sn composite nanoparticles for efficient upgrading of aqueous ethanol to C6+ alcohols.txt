3. Results and discussion 
3.1. Structure of the catalysts 
NiSn@C/TiO2 was prepared via a chemical approach by in situ 
assembling active Ni-Sn nanoparticles into a hybrid support constituting 
lipophilic carbon shell and hydrophilic TiO2 (see detailed information in 
experimental section). In particular, citric acid was used as reductant, 
surface ligand, as well as the percussor for the carbonation of Ni-Sn 
nanoparticles, while TiO2 was used directly to modify the hydrophilia 
of the catalyst. NiSn@C/TiO2 was supposed to be amphiphilic due to the 
hybrid support (Scheme 1). Here, NiSn@

the oil phase (Fig. 3k,l). The above results clearly demonstrate that the 
lipophilic and hydrophilic properties of catalysts can be precisely 
modulated by altering the molar ratio of C/TiO2.
