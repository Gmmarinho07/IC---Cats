5. Conclusions
The main conclusions from these data are the following:
1. Acidic catalysts like zeolites, alumina and silica–alumina are
active in the temperature range 453–573 K for both ethanol
dehydration to DEE and to ethylene and for DEE cracking and
hydrolysis, producing back ethanol and ethylene.
2. Protonic zeolites are more active than alumina which is slightly
more active than silica–alumina for these reactions. It is con-
ﬁrmed that the active sites of silica–alumina are different and
less active than those of protonic zeolites.
3. Ethanol dehydration to DEE occurs selectively at lower tem-
perature with a ‘‘bimolecular’’ mechanism involving reaction
of ethoxy groups with undissociated ethanol.
