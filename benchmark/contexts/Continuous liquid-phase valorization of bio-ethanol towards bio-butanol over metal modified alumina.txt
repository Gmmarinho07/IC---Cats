Results and discussion
3.1. Evaluation of catalytic properties of Co-, Ni- and Cu-modiﬁed
alumina for valorization of bio-ethanol to bio-butanol
Based on extensive screening among different metal loadings as
well as calcination and reduction temperatures, the best metals
with corresponding loadings are presented here. The model cata-
lyst (HTC-500, 20.7 wt-% Ni/Al2O3, Crossﬁeld) presented before [33]
was included for comparison with self-prepared alumina catalysts
from prescreening results [33]. As a result of extensive screening,
the three metals presented here were found to be the best metals
when aiming at producing 1-butanol (Cu, Ni) or ethyl acetate (Co).
The evaluation of the three most promising metals (Table 1) was
carried out by varying the metal loading and calcination (Cu) or
reduction temperature (Ni, Co). The optimized copper loadings
used were at 1.8- and 4.5 wt-%, respectively (marked as Cu-1.8 and
Cu-4.5), cobalt at 16 wt-% (Co-16) and nickel at 19 wt-% (Ni-19). The
targeted and measured metal amounts are reported in Table 2.

for copper catalysts was in the range of 400 C, whereas for cobalt
and nickel, the suitable reduction temperature was 500 C. Inter-
